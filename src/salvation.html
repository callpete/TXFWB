How to become a Christian

The original page presents a salvation message centered on human sin, the gift of eternal life through Jesus Christ, repentance, belief, confession of Jesus as Lord, and the importance of telling others about that decision.

Core outline

  1. Every person is separated from God by sin.
  2. Salvation is presented as God’s gift through Jesus Christ.
  3. The response described is repentance, belief, and confessing Jesus as Lord.
  4. New believers are encouraged to tell others, be baptized, read the Bible, pray, and attend church faithfully.
